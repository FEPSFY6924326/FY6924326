Abstract

This paper explores mathematics and artificial intelligence, especially from a contemporary medical perspective.
At the same time, AI is often imaged as enigmatic meaning it is hard to interpret and understand, thus much of it
springs from maths - for example; optimisation,probability and geometry. it shows, through and appllication illustrated
by an everyday screening example, how "maths and ai coolide" when we make real-world choices, once we've descirbed some 
of the fundemental points. the main aim is to highlight the pros and cons to consider whether ai in medicine is approriate
and to consider these points when making a final judgement
